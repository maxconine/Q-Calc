- Native only (2026-10-26): `iπ`, `i π`, `eπ` multiply instead of reading as calls `i(pi)`/`e (pi)`, and `exp` takes a complex argument, so `e^(iπ)+1` and `exp(i pi)+1` give 0. The TS app still answers blank / "undefined". Tests: `core/tests/native_fixes.rs`.
- Native only (2026-10-27, found by `bench/fuzz.rs`; tests in `core/tests/robustness.rs`). The TS engine has each of these too:
  - Brackets past a depth no longer recurse without end: the unit reader stops at 1,000 and the formula reader at 5,000. `((((…` 20,000 deep overflowed the stack and killed the app; in TS it throws a RangeError.
  - `angles::calls_worked_out` evaluates at most 40,000 characters per line: a deep nest of calls that never evaluates (`abs(abs(…(-1)…))` 1,000 deep) was quadratic, 13 s natively and 3 s in TS.
  - `symbolic::simplify` has a work budget (12M units of rule matching and tree walks, 26× the most a golden fixture spends): `d/dx x^x^…^x` past ten x's, or `d/dx` typed thirty times, took seconds per level and gigabytes, and `isolate x in x^5 + x = y y y …` (500 y's) 11 s. Such lines are now blank; TS still answers `d/dx` typed 8 times over `x^x`, in about a second.
  - `blank_reason`'s stray-operator search evaluates at most 40,000 characters: it retried the whole line once per operator, so `e^/^/…` 1,000 characters long took 6 s live (the TS reader does the same). `missing_unit` likewise stops after 20,000 (14 tries per bare numerator: 0.9 s on `asin(5.2331 / 791 / 791 / …)`).
  - nCr and nPr past 2^53: `nCr(999999999999999999999, 4)` looped forever (mathjs's balanced loop counts to n by 1) and `nPr(1e17, 100)` recursed until the stack overflowed, in TS too. They now take the running product (4.17e82; overflow), and `nPr(1e20, 3)` is 1e60 where TS says 1e20.
  - `inclusiveRange` stops after its count: `inclusiveRange(2^53+1, 2^53)` pushed 2^53 forever (2^53 + 1 is 2^53), in TS too.
  - Derivatives are capped at 50,000 nodes written out (the golden ones have under 100), and simplifyCore no longer copies each subtree at every level; `d/dx` ten times over a 6,500-letter product took 8.7 s and 5.5 GB (0.7 s and 39 MB now).
  - Port bugs the TS never had, fixed natively: gcd loops kept going on NaN where JS `while (b)` stops (`lcm(2^1023, 1e300, 0.1)` hung); the precise twin's dashu `exp`/`pow`/`asinh`/`atanh` took seconds (and hundreds of MB) on arguments like 1e-123456789, now rounded to 1 or x directly when the series can't reach the last digit.
  - Graph options are bounded: a contour’s aspect is kept within 1/16…16 (an infinite or 1e9 one, as a panel of no width would give, asked for a 116 TB grid and aborted), and a curve takes at most 100,000 samples (an infinite count looped forever). The Swift panel never sends such values today; the TS has neither limit.
