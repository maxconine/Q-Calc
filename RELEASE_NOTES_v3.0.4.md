## Q Calc 3.0.4: more words, clearer hints, and a reworked fluids sheet

**If you already have Q Calc on a Mac, you don't need to do anything.** It updates itself to 3.0.4 the usual way, and your settings, keybinds and history come with it.

This is a Mac release. Windows stays on 3.0.2 for now.

### New

- **Algebra in words.** `simplify (x^2-1)/(x-1)` is `x + 1`, `expand (x+1)^2`, `factor 12` is `2² × 3`, `factor x^2 + 5x + 6`, `factors of 28`, `is 91 prime`.
- **More words.** `log base 2 of 8`, `sum(1 to 10)`, `mean 1 2 3`, `average of 1, 2, 3`, `sqrt of 16`, `1/2 of 10`, plus `fib` and `mode`.
- **Units in calls.** `round(5.5 m)`, `floor`, `ceil`, `min(1 ft, 2 m)`, `max`, `sum`, `mean` and `median` keep their units.
- **Fuel economy.** `40 mpg to L/100km` and `8 L/100km to km/L`.
- **Clock sums and money.** `3:30 + 1:45` is `5:15`, `$40 * 15%` is `$6.00`, and `now + (.8*2hr)` answers.
- **`7k` is 7000** everywhere, including `x = 7k` (it was 7 kelvin).
- **Bigger sheet pictures.** Hover a picture and click its magnifier to see it enlarged.
- **Fluids, reworked.** The continuum mechanics sheet splits fluids into three sections, with pictures of uniform flow, pipe flow and a boundary layer.
- **A download for the disk image.** Each release now has a drag-to-Applications `.dmg` beside the zip.

### Fixes

- More ↵ hints for lines that can't be answered: `5 x 3`, `x = 5 = 6`, `1.000,5`, `300 K + 5`, lists of different lengths, and a unit inside `sin` or a power.
- An equation's explanation card no longer sits under the bar, and you can drag it off into its own window.
- `integral rho V · d A` previews as maths.
- The `ans` chip sits on the line instead of above it.
- `pi r^2` no longer reads r as rankine.
- The bar says when a newer Q Calc is available.
